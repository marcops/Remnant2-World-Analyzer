About Remnant 2 World Analyzer

A free save analyzer for Remnant 2, the sibling of Remnant World Analyzer (Remnant: From the Ashes). Load your save and it shows every collectible of the game and its DLCs — The Awakened King, The Forgotten Kingdom and The Dark Horizon — as had or missing: weapons, mods, mutators, armor pieces, amulets, rings, relics, relic fragments, traits, archetypes, prisms, concoctions, consumables and quest items, each with its icon, what it does and how to get it.

The list of items is read from the game's own files (the same paths the save uses), checked against a community item database and the wiki. It is free, needs no install or account, and works in any browser.

What it shows

  • Current world — your campaign or adventure: every area, its bosses, dungeons, events, rings and amulets on the ground, trait books and simulacrums, what each drops and whether you have it (✔) or not (✘), and what you already picked up there.
  • Map — the areas of each world linked as in the game, the part of each area you have explored, and what is still lying there.
  • World state — difficulty, time played, respawn point, quest items carried, areas visited and events done per world.
  • Tips — what to do next: simulacrums and trait books still in your world, items you don't have that can drop there, what to buy from each vendor.
  • Items — every collectible as a picture, filtered by what you have or don't have, type and world/DLC; click one for what it does, how to get it and its wiki page.
  • Checklist — "x of y" with percentages by item type and by world, and the list of what you're missing with how to get each one.
  • Traits & archetypes — every trait and archetype with your level in each, your trait rank and trait points.
  • My character — archetypes, power level, item level, Dragon Heart and your equipped gear with upgrade levels.
  • Build & DPS — damage per second of your guns and damage per hit of your melee weapons at their upgrade level, from the wiki's stats, and what they reach at max level.
  • Raw data — everything stored in your save files, browsable.
  • Save editor — change your scrap, irons, Lumenite, Relic Dust, Tomes of Knowledge and trait points. With Start.bat it saves to the game folder (game closed, folder backed up first); on the website it downloads the edited profile.sav.

Where are the Remnant 2 save files?

On PC (Steam) they are in %USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Remnant2\Steam\<number>. profile.sav holds your characters and everything they own; save_N.sav is the world of character N+1.

Does it update while I play?

Run it locally with Start.bat (Windows): it finds your save folder by itself and refreshes the page every time the game saves. In the browser, drop the files again to refresh.

Are my save files uploaded?

No. The files are read by your own browser; nothing is sent to any server.
